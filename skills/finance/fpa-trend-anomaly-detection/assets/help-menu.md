# Trend Analysis and Anomaly Detection: help

Reviews any numbers over time: where each line is heading, and which periods break its own pattern.

**What you provide**
- source: a spreadsheet or CSV, an open session tab, or numbers pasted in chat
- analysis: trend, anomaly, or both (default: both)
- thresholds: your materiality rule, for example "5% and 50,000" (default: 10%)

**What you get**
- Trends: direction, annual rate, year on year, last twelve months, changes in trend, and steps in level
- Anomalies: each unusual period with its amount, percent, and type (one-off, temporary, new level), plus data checks
- An interactive dashboard, and an Excel workbook for tracing and sign-off when you ask for one

**Try saying**
- "How is revenue trending in this file?"
- "Find anomalies in our monthly expense lines, 5% and 50,000."
- "Anything unusual this month before close?"
- "Is this March spike a blip or a trend?"

It knows only each line's own history, so it says what moved, not why. Outputs are informational and are not accounting or financial advice. Validate with a qualified accountant or finance controller before distributing or acting on them.
