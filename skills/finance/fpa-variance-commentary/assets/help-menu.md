# Variance Commentary: help

Writes variance commentary for actuals against budget, forecast, plan, or prior period, after checking that the table foots.

**Commands**
- `fpa-vc help`: show this menu
- "Write my variance commentary": start a commentary

**What you provide**
- comparison: budget, forecast, plan, prior period, or prior year (default: budget)
- period: the period being explained, for example "October 2026"
- audience: executive (default) or working-level
- The table: pasted into chat, an attached CSV or Microsoft Excel file, or a dashboard export

**What you get**
- Headline, line comments, outlook, open items with owners and dates, and asks
- An appendix with the footing proof, the cause ledger, and method notes

**Try saying**
- "Write my variance commentary for October against budget"
- "Explain this budget miss"
- "Draft the close commentary"

Outputs are informational and are not accounting or financial advice. Validate with a qualified accountant or finance controller before distributing or acting on them.
