# <Skill name>: design document (example)

This is an example. Fill in what you know, leave the rest for later, and update it as the skill changes.

## Overview

The problem, who has it, and what the skill does about it.

## Requirements

- Business: the outcome or decision the skill supports, and how you'll know it's met.
- Functional: what the skill does for each kind of request, and when data or a dependency is missing.
- Non-functional: accuracy, response time, data access, cost, and other limits, if they apply.

## Design

- Architecture: the workflows, and what the agent, the tools, and the scripts each do.
- Data: what each source holds, and the fields and queries the skill uses.
- Interfaces: the inputs and their defaults, and what the answer contains.
- Dependencies: the spaces, connectors, tools, and skills it needs.
- Alternatives considered: the other approaches, and why you didn't choose them.

## Acceptance

How you'll check the finished skill: the requests you'll try on real data, and what a right answer looks like.

## Out of scope

What the skill deliberately doesn't do, and why.
