# Research Prospect Workflow

This skill researches a named company and produces a prospect brief.

## Data sources

- Public web research (10-K filings, earnings releases, company pages)
- CRM lookup (Account, Opportunity, Contact records)

## Output format

The skill produces either:
- A visual widget (when `display_widget` is available)
- Structured markdown (terminal/text-only clients)
